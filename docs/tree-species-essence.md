Douglas-fir's original and chunky V1 renderings share a botanical identity; the four oak forms remain variants of one species. No runtime metadata or ecological records are added.

## Current five: pre-V3 review

The latest skeleton repair establishes credible attachments but does not by itself establish species essence. Mangrove roots are readable close up yet subordinate at forest distance. Avicennia's leaders remain too balanced. Cacao's leaf masses can obscure its pods and low branching. Hevea still reads as upward fans over a fork rather than a generous crown. These are composition issues; extra leaf counts are not the remedy.

V3 is one profile pass using existing height, spread, crown-radius distribution, curvature, tropism, overlap/cluster-size and root-spread settings. One optional local crown-lean control supplies Avicennia's directional gesture; it is not simulated environmental wind. Leaf atlases, materials, scene lighting and other species are protected.

The first comparison is recorded below. No subsequent polish has been performed.

### Parameters used in this bounded pass

Only the five profiles in `src/lib/studio/species-v1.ts` changed. Branch, root and leaf counts were not increased. Existing radius, leader spread/top, primary/secondary rise, apical bias, tropism, cluster size, dominance and root reach/height express the revised compositions. The one added optional `branching.crownLean` shifts all leader goals coherently in local space; only Avicennia enables it. Branch origins and their attached foliage follow those paths. It is not a foliage-only offset or an environmental simulation. Cacao's jorquette and all existing foliage atlases, batching, shaders, palettes and lighting remain unchanged.

### Deferred V2/V3 backlog — approval needed before another pass

1. **Crown gesture:** Stylosa's fan-shaped top, Hevea's exposed Y and Avicennia's separated terminal still weaken identity. A future experiment should adjust leader/secondary hierarchy rather than add leaves.
2. **Foundation readability:** Mangrove root arches are broader but too fine at forest distance; Avicennia's breathing-root field remains subtle. Any later thickness/rhythm experiment must keep grounded attachment and avoid decorative radial arrangements.
3. **Secondary masses:** Cacao needs readable layered depth and selective pod exposure, not a thicker flat hedge. The same review should check whether the two mangroves retain sufficient internal crown depth at oblique angles.
