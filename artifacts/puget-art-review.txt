Wildloom painterly environment — first integrated art comparison

Changed: environment-foundations.ts, painted-renderer.ts; added prepare-puget-art.cjs and capture-puget-art.cjs.
Asset library: public/assets/puget-v1 — meadow, forest-floor, path, grass and foliage PNGs, all 2048 square.
Original generated source PNGs: artifacts/puget-art-sources. Generation: built-in image_gen, five separate calls.
Prompts requested independent richly detailed gouache game albedos: meadow moss/emerald/lime fine vegetation;
forest floor umber soil, moss, small fallen leaves and twigs; warm dirt with tiny stones; four upright grass
clusters in four columns on true alpha; eight ferns/shrubs/leafy plants/white and purple flowers in a 4x2 alpha grid.
All prompts excluded labels, large landmarks, backgrounds on atlases, cast shadows and directional baked lighting.
No reference-sheet crops. Sources resampled to 2K; this is not a claim of native 2K generation.
Ground preparation uses paired cosine border blending with exact shared edge pixels (96px strips).
Atlas preparation insets each column/cell with 48px horizontal / 64px vertical padding; generated alpha retained.
Generation itself cannot guarantee physically unlit pigments or final production quality; these assets need art review.
Ground silhouettes are generic decorative imagery and do not add ecological species records.

Materials use sRGB texture storage, linear mipmaps, linear magnification and anisotropy 4.
Three world-space ground scales (4 / 3.5 / 3m), smooth soil/litter masks and macro pigment variation.
Grass retains two instanced batches with stable seed stream, varied scale/yaw/lean/tint and distance reduction;
art mode increases width/height and acceptance near trees and side/perimeter areas while reserving the clearing.
Sun is warmer and stronger; cool fill is reduced; shader foreground shadows deepen while highlights stay selective.
Tree geometry, ecological data, seeds, camera, placement, sky/cloud generation and UI were not edited.
