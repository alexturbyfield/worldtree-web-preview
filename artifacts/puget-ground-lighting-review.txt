GroundCompositionLighting is an optional groundLighting field on EncounterCompositionProfile.
Controls: foregroundDarkness .34, sideFraming .30, focalCenter optional world X/Z (defaults to the projected
clearing center), focalWidth [23,29] world-space scale, transitionSoftness .8, warmth .2, irregularity .3.
Use a customized profile via mountPaintedForest(..., {environment:{composition:profile}}).
The field uses a fixed world basis captured from the encounter composition, broad smooth transitions and
seeded low-frequency sine warping. It does not reference tree positions, shadow maps, view matrices or time.
Foreground and side weights combine smoothly; the broad warm opening is not a circular spotlight.

The same GLSL field is applied to terrain, crossed/single-card grass instances, short grass geometry and
existing small ground vegetation. It modulates light after material shading, preserving texture/vertex pigment
detail. The field has a bounded darkening weight and a pigment-relative green bounce floor, avoiding nearly
black meadow shadows. No new textures/assets/geometry, full-screen filter or postprocessing pass.
The debug query overrides surface lighting with false color: teal = framing shade, yellow = opening.
Tree crowns and sky remain normally shaded in that view. Debug is temporary and adds no UI.

Physical shadows remain separate: real alpha-masked tree shadow maps are unchanged, while B caps shadow
intensity at .48 (A .90), raises hemisphere fill from .75 to at least .95 and uses cool green ground fill
#6c8f75. Tree shadow silhouettes and directional sunlight remain visible; no trees were moved to drive the field.
Existing broad cloud transmission stays separate and unchanged.

Validation: deterministic field output; changing tree positions does not change the field; camera motion after
resolution does not move the captured field; foreground-darkness control changes the field without mutating defaults.
A/B/debug camera arrays, tree position-buffer fingerprints and sky bitmap hashes match exactly.
All browser/shader error arrays are empty. TypeScript passes; git diff whitespace check passes.
All views: 17 draw calls / 713359 submitted triangles; instancing and instance counts unchanged.
Desktop Chromium / ANGLE SwiftShader at pixel ratio .65; physical mobile/GPU timing not measured.
