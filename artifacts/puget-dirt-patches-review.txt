Grass-broken dirt patches and corner rock
Files: landscape-detail.ts (seeded mask), environment-foundations.ts (material blend), encounter.ts (hero rock x -13 to -15.5m).
A 512px numeric mask covers the existing 100m ground. Domain-warped Worley F1 plus three-octave value-noise fBM defines coverage; a second channel gives broad pigment variation. It is stable in world coordinates and generated once per seed. Painted grass blade highlights erode the mask edge, retaining irregular grass fingers and islands rather than feathered circles alone. Existing painted path texture supplies soil grain, graded into saturated warm ochres that receive the same approved vignette, focal illumination, lights and shadows as grass.
No terrain or vegetation geometry, tree placement, camera, sky, models or ecological data changes. The large corner rock moves left 2.5m; its height follows the same terrain function. No draw calls or triangles added: 19 calls, 675089 triangles before and after. One mask texture and the existing dirt texture are sampled; extra texture memory and shader cost were not GPU-benchmarked.
Validation: TypeScript passed; mask reproduced identically for seed29 with a nonconstant [0,208] scalar range. No browser/shader errors in final capture. Tree and sky fingerprints, camera, exposure, shadow strength, vignette, illumination and existing ground instance data match prior scene.
Comparison puget-dirt-patches-comparison.png; scalar mask puget-dirt-mask.png. Current encounter enables patches by default. dirtPatches=off disables only the soil blend for comparison. This is artistic surface dressing, not a measured soil map. No deployment.
