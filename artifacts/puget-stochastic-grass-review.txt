Stochastic grass tiling
Source change: src/lib/forest/environment-foundations.ts only.
Preserves the accepted texture generator and palette. World X/Z coordinates form a triangular lattice. Each lattice vertex hashes scene seed and cell identity into an offset and quarter-turn rotation. Three samples blend with cubed, normalized barycentric weights; shared vertices give continuous transitions between triangles. Sharpened blending limits loss of painted detail.
textureGrad uses unmodified world-UV derivatives transformed by each tile rotation. This prevents random offsets/discrete cell changes from selecting incorrect mip levels. Existing mipmaps and anisotropic filtering remain enabled. Hashes are deterministic per world cell, with no time or camera input; last-bit procedural hash variation between GPU implementations is possible.
Three grass fetches replace one; no new draw calls, textures, mesh buffers, instances, scenery, light changes or render targets. GPU cost was not benchmarked. Browser rendering remains 18 calls / 560785 triangles in both matched captures. Geometry fingerprint, sky hash, camera, exposure, physical shadow strength, vignette, illumination and ground instance data match. Shader/page errors absent. TypeScript passed.
