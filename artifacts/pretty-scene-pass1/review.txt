Major parameters
Serializable deterministic patch plan; independent presentation seed suffix. No source trees, ecological
selection, tree architecture, structural seeds or species data changed. No new textures or dependencies.
World-space elliptical masses with seeded angle, radii and strength coordinate plants and ground color.
Savanna: 9 patches, radii roughly 5-8 m x 6-11 m; clear ellipse center [3,-4], radii [12,20] m.
Rainforest: 10 patches, radii roughly 3.4-6.4 m x 4-9 m; clear ellipse center [0,2], radii [5.2,10] m.
Both protect the near approach as well as the encounter opening. Asymmetric foreground and lateral
plant masses supply physical framing; no screen-space vignette was added.
Savanna: 4,200 alpha-tested grass cards; 35 existing shrubs redistributed. Existing 2,800 procedural
grass instances retained but hidden. One batched grass draw, one 108-triangle escarpment mesh.
Rainforest: redistribute 2,142 existing fern, palm, shrub, broadleaf and foliage-card instances.
Existing epiphytes, lianas, litter/root decals, forest-wall depth layers and tree placement stay in place.
Hero features: existing left acacia (12.55 m tall) and left buttressed rainforest canopy tree (44.57 m).
Sun-facing upper crown accents have 13 m / 7 m horizontal influence, preserving shaded interiors.
Sun color #ffe7bc; hemisphere fill savanna .62, rainforest .68. Existing physical shadows retained.
Savanna ground palette soil #9a7951, vegetation #8f964f, sun #c8b36c, cool shade #657568.
Rainforest ground palette soil #5c4934, vegetation #4c6542, sun #b8a35f, cool shade #294d47.
Savanna atmospheric near/far 95/280 m; foliage atmosphere 110/260 m. Rainforest retains V5 depth fog.

Review caveats — this is the first prototype, not approved final art
The savanna's card grass reads too short/dark and leaves the near ground under-framed. Its escarpment
is visibly faceted and needs a more convincing material/depth treatment. The rainforest floor is
warmer and calmer but existing decal silhouettes now stand out against it; the elevated inspection
still exposes the finite forest because encounter-only backdrop layers hide above 18 m eye height.
Selected crown accents remain subtle; foreground foliage is still coarse. No second visual iteration
was made after the comparison. These are review findings, not changes already implemented.
