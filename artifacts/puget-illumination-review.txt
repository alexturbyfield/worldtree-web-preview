EncounterCompositionProfile accepts optional PainterlyIllumination in illumination.
Default controls: enabled true; focalStrength .9; optional focalCenter world X/Z (defaults to projected
clearing center shifted 5m into the middle ground); focalWidth [19,20]; softness .8; warmth .5;
irregularity .35; patchCount 3 (bounded to 4); patchStrength .38; crownStrength .5.
Pass a custom profile through mountPaintedForest(..., {environment:{composition:profile}}).

The illumination field is independent of GroundCompositionLighting: its strength/shape/patches can change
without altering the dark vignette. It uses a captured world basis, seeded region placement and broad,
domain-warped feathering. Three overlapping regions add soft light/shade variation, without a screen-space
spotlight, texture replacement, bloom, scenery, new assets or global exposure adjustment.
The warm multiplier preserves underlying painterly detail. Foreground protection fades out added illumination
before the near foreground, retaining existing cool green shade and the readable bounce floor.
The same field covers terrain, instanced grass cards, short grass geometry and small ground vegetation.

Only existing compositionally selected trees receive new crown accents. Their shader masks combine selected
crown volume, actual sun-facing normals, upper/exterior height and background quieting. Lower/interior foliage
keeps its existing deep-green shading. crownStrength controls the accents. This is an art-directed light-response
multiplier, not a new physical light or emissive glow. The directional sun, shadow map, shadow strength .72,
exposure 1.12 and ground vignette are unchanged. Tree placement/model buffers, 18m foreground exclusion,
species/population data, sky, clouds, camera, textures, UI and inspection behavior are preserved.
