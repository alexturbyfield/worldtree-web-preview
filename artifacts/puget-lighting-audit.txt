Audit BEFORE:

Tree crowns were custom ShaderMaterials with hard-coded (-.6,.85,.55) key lighting and fixed pigment response;
changing the actual scene light did not control this response. Grass used unlit Basic materials.
Only trunks/rocks cast shadows; library canopy cards did not. Shadows were PCF (Three's replacement for
legacy PCFSoft), static, 1024 square in software/mobile and 2048 on desktop, normal bias .12 / bias -.0003.

Pass 1 correction:
Sun (-36,38,22), warm #ffe0a8, intensity 4.8, upper-left in the preserved camera. Fill .18;
hemisphere .75 with cool sky #a3c4df and ground #52726a. Exposure and tone mapping unchanged.
The encounter library foliage shader now receives live world-space sun direction, color and intensity.
Transferred cluster normals drive N dot L, crown pigment selection and warm direct irradiance;
a cool nonzero shading floor retains interior detail. Foliage atlas and geometry remain unchanged.
Canopy cards now cast static alpha-masked shadows using their existing atlas rectangles. Grass uses lit
Lambert materials and receives shadows. Canopy self-shadow reception is not implemented; interiors use
transferred normals and existing interior pigment data. Shadow refresh remains static during this seeded scene.
Fog remains 76–175m; no added fog conceals geometry.

Quantitative material check (foliage-only renders; mean absolute 8-bit RGB change across the canvas):
Sun off: 8.170; horizontal sun reversed: 12.095.
Both exceed the capture assertion threshold of 2.0. This verifies light intensity and direction affect the crowns.
The capture isolates foliage to avoid measuring ground/wood response instead. Camera/exposure are unchanged.
This proof applies to the library foliage used throughout this encounter; unrelated legacy fallback shaders retain their existing behavior.

The third diagnostic panel adds an art-directed irregular canopy-opening mask to DIRECT diffuse irradiance,
projected along the actual sun vector at 12m height. It is not an albedo brightening or exposure adjustment.
The opening keeps the clearing sunlit with shaded framing edges, combined with real canopy shadow maps.
Textures and placements are identical in the three diagnostic panels. No new assets or props were generated.
