IMPLEMENTATION — THREE SOURCE FILES
src/lib/forest/composition-archetype.ts:
  Added an Amazon-only V4 stratum configuration and bounded greedy canopy occupancy (81 samples, 18 candidates
  per main-canopy placement). Candidates cover poorly occupied crown footprints while excluding central trunks.
  2 near-edge main-canopy trunks, 4 emergents, 26 other main-canopy trees, 12 subcanopy and 20 understory instances.
  Uniform scene-instance scales reuse existing source models/branch architecture; no foliage-density inflation,
  no botanical profile/generator changes, and no new ecological species. The V3 archetype remains accessible.
src/lib/forest/biome-visual.ts:
  Amazon-only configuration: 64 visual composition instances, forest-floor texture at 5m repeat, warmer brown/umber
  material pigment, grass disabled, no horizon hill, fog delayed from 25–90m to 65–150m with deep green depth.
  Sun [-28,48,18], warm intensity 3.8, ambient .42; existing focal painterly illumination and warm foliage response
  reused. Puget and other biome configurations remain on their existing branches.
  Added six shared GPU-instanced life-form batches: broadleaf seedlings, fern clusters, pinnate-frond palms,
  shrubs, lianas and trunk epiphytes. Geometry is shared per batch, with deterministic size/yaw/tint and tree-base
  or foreground-edge patches. The central opening rejects tall ground dressing. No grass carpet or rocks added.
  Existing integrated mahogany buttresses scale with the source composition instances; no invented kapok model.
  Replaced broad hard shaft planes with two narrow, softly feathered additive shaft hints.
src/lib/forest/painted-renderer.ts:
  Amazon V4-only camera clamp correction and transition through the existing inspection route. This preserves
  the same composed V4 layout for inspection; the existing far LOD remains active there. Puget camera/lighting untouched.

FIRST-RENDER REVIEW / MISSING ASSETS AND LIMITATIONS
The human view now reads taller and more enclosed overhead, with massive cropped edge trunks and recognizable
broadleaf/palm/fern layers. The forest floor is brown leaf litter, and a central encounter space remains readable.
Acceptance is still partial: rear sky windows/terrain boundary remain exposed, rear enclosure is insufficient,
understory remains graphic, some crown and ground texture motifs repeat, and selective illumination is weaker
than the supplied photographic concept. The inspection view exposes the finite terrain slab and canopy holes.
No extra fog was added to conceal these gaps. Stop after this first comparison instead of polishing further.
The existing Puget forest-floor PNG is reused/tinted; a dedicated Amazon litter/soil asset is still missing.
Life-form plants are unnamed procedural dressing, not species-specific botanical reconstructions. Better tropical
leaf surfaces/atlas art, branch-aware vine/epiphyte attachment and larger detailed buttress surfaces remain missing.
Shafts are view-dependent planar hints, not physically occluded volumetric scattering. No bloom is added.
