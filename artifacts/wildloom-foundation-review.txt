Wildloom environment foundation pass — review

Deliverables (in artifacts/):

  wildloom-foundation-grass-study.png — four atlas variants and nine sampled instanced tufts.
  wildloom-foundation-tree-hierarchy.png — identical maple and Douglas-fir specimens at three treatment depths.

  Raw tile, atlas and individual comparison PNGs are retained beside these sheets.

Implementation:
  src/lib/forest/environment-foundations.ts (new) — reusable periodic ground, independent world masks,
    grass atlas/instancing/LOD, shader color hierarchy and optional spatial focal weight.
  src/lib/forest/encounter.ts — installs foundations while retaining previous dressing as a fallback;
    preserves old random stream for existing sky, shrubs and rocks.
  src/lib/forest/painted-renderer.ts — connects the foundation hook and grades shared tree materials.
  scripts/studies/environment-foundations.ts and capture-environment-foundations.cjs — reproducible review captures.
  No dependencies, species, UI, tree generators, leaf atlas designs, terrain geometry or scene placement were added/changed.

Ground: 256×256 base-color tile, repeating every 7 m; periodic broad pigment washes.
  Soil and vegetation fields are separate seeded world-space functions. Large regions do not come from the tile.
Grass: padded 512×128 atlas, four variants; deterministic scale, yaw, lean, tint and clumped acceptance.
  Elliptical clearing reserves the encounter center with only 2.5% residual acceptance before other masks.
  Near: two crossed cards / 4 triangles per tuft, fades at 32–46 m.
  Far: ~30% stable single-card subset / 2 triangles per tuft, fades in at 30–44 m and out at 68–88 m.
  Both batches are submitted; vertex culling and alpha masking remove distant surfaces on the GPU.
Trees: species pigments remain inputs; smooth world-space ramps add deep foreground greens/selective warmth,
  modest midground contrast/saturation reduction, and cooler/narrower background color.
  Atmosphere matches Three.js fog depth and output color space; the grading axis stays fixed between cameras.
  Optional API: mountPaintedForest(..., {environment:{focal:{treeIndex:0,weight:0.8}}}).
  Fallback API: mountPaintedForest(..., {environment:false}). Baseline and Diagram rendering are unchanged.

Caveats: this is a foundation pass, not completed reference-level composition or mobile optimization.
  The existing terrain box still intersects the synthetic terrain as a tan patch; terrain geometry was preserved.
  Grass silhouettes remain graphic and can show crossed-card roots close up; they do not receive tree shadows.
  A focal ellipsoid can affect adjacent overlapping crowns. No deployment, commit or push was performed.
