# Foliage study
- Work on one species and one seed first.
- Keep camera, lighting, tree scale, and palette fixed so changes are comparable.
- Match reference foliage: full canopy volume, layered clumps, irregular leaf or needle silhouettes, dark interiors, bright exterior highlights.
- Avoid bare skeletons, uniformly noisy cauliflower crowns, perfect spheres, and thousands of tiny leaf cards.
- Change one of: clump arrangement, silhouette tufts, atlas texture, or shading, per experiment.
- Keep procedural variation deterministic and separate structural vs appearance seeds.
- Preserve diagram/previous rendering mode as fallback.
- Prefer a single tree-lab preview over rebuilding the full forest.
- Only optimize if a measured bottleneck is visible.
